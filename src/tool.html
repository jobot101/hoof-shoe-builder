Getting it to the Bambu from your phone
  1. Tap the saved zip in Files to unzip it. You get the STL plus print notes.
  2. On makerworld.com, upload the STL as a private model.
  3. In Bambu Handy, open your models, pick it, choose your TPU, and print.

Handy slices in the cloud with stock settings. For tuned TPU settings, slice it once in Bambu Studio and attach that 3MF as the model's print profile; prints from Handy then use it.
